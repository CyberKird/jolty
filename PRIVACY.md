# Jolty privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Jolty is a desktop app that runs on your own PC. It has no servers of its own and no account with us. Your chats, files and settings stay on your PC unless you choose to back them up.

## Sign in with Google (optional)

You can connect your Google account so Jolty can keep an encrypted backup of its data in Google Drive. Jolty asks for these permissions:

- `drive.appdata`: a hidden app folder in your Google Drive that only Jolty can see. Jolty cannot read, change or delete any of your other Drive files.
- `openid`, `email`, `profile`: to show which Google account is connected.

What is stored in that folder: one file with your Jolty personalization (skills, commands, instructions, settings, the list of accounts without their keys, schedules, tool connections) and the time you have used Jolty. It is encrypted on your PC with a password only you know before it leaves your PC. We cannot read it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Jolty does not send your Google data, your chats or any usage data to us or to any third party. The Google sign-in tokens are kept on your PC, encrypted with Windows protection of your user account. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup feature you see in the app.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other services

Jolty talks to the AI providers and services you connect yourself (for example Claude, ChatGPT or an API key you add). What they receive and keep is governed by their own policies.

## Stop and delete

In Jolty, Settings, Account, you can disconnect Google at any time. You can also remove Jolty's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the hidden Jolty folder data from your Drive settings.
